# 📊 Telecom Plan Revenue Analysis  
Statistical Comparison of Prepaid Plans – Megaline

---

## 📖 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer usage behavior and revenue generation for two prepaid telecom plans (Surf and Ultimate) offered by Megaline.

The objective is to determine which plan generates higher average revenue and provide statistical evidence to support marketing budget allocation decisions.

The analysis is based on a dataset of 500 customers, including call usage, text messaging, internet consumption, and monthly subscription data for the year 2018.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

- Analyze customer usage patterns across prepaid plans  
- Calculate monthly revenue per user  
- Compare revenue distribution between plans  
- Perform statistical hypothesis testing to determine if revenue differences are significant  

---

## 🗂 Dataset Description

The project uses five datasets:

- **users** – customer demographic and subscription information  
- **calls** – call duration records  
- **messages** – SMS usage data  
- **internet** – internet session data  
- **plans** – plan pricing and limits  

---

## 🛠️ Methodology

### 1️⃣ Data Preparation
- Aggregation of monthly usage per user  
- Revenue calculation based on plan limits and overage fees  
- Handling missing values  
- Data type corrections  

### 2️⃣ Revenue Calculation
- Monthly subscription fee  
- Additional charges for exceeding call, message, and data limits  
- Plan-specific rounding rules applied  

### 3️⃣ Statistical Analysis
- Revenue distribution comparison  
- Hypothesis testing (t-test)  
- Significance level evaluation  
- Interpretation of p-values  

---

## 📈 Key Findings

- Revenue distribution differs between the Surf and Ultimate plans.  
- Usage patterns significantly impact additional charges.  
- Statistical testing indicates whether the difference in average revenue is significant.  
- Results support data-driven marketing budget allocation decisions.
